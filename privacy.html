Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 Effective Date: July 18, 2026 Last Updated: July 18, 2026

1. Overview

We respect your privacy. This application does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

2. Data We Store

We only store data that you explicitly create, upload, or modify while using the application, including configuration data, files, and application content.

3. User Control

You have full control over your data. You may create, update, or delete your data at any time within the application.

4. Data Usage

Data is used solely for providing application functionality and is not used for analytics, marketing, or profiling.

5. Technical Information

For security and operational purposes, basic technical information such as IP address and request logs may be temporarily processed.

6. Data Storage

Data is stored in secure databases and file storage systems managed by the application infrastructure.

7. Third-Party Services

If applicable, third-party services, such as authentication or payment providers, may process limited data required for their functionality.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user data with third parties except where necessary for the operation and delivery of the service.

9. Technical Data and Logging

We collect technical logs when you use our platform. These logs may include application events, user actions, error reports, and performance data.

This information is used solely to maintain, secure, and improve the service, including debugging issues and optimizing performance.

We do not use this data for advertising or user profiling, and we do not sell user data to third parties.

Some logs may temporarily include user inputs when necessary for troubleshooting technical issues.

10. Communications

We may send you service-related communications, including alerts, security notices, system updates, and transactional messages related to your use of the platform.

11. Payments

Payments are processed through third-party providers such as Stripe. We may share necessary billing information with these providers to process transactions.

12. Cookies and Local Storage

The Service may use essential cookies and local storage to enable authentication, maintain sessions, and ensure proper functionality.

No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

13. Product Updates

We may send users emails regarding new features, improvements, and product updates.

Users may opt out of non-essential product update communications by sending an email to contact@booleancomputing.com or by using the unsubscribe link provided in such emails.
